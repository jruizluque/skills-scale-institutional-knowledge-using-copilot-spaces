# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads ensure product quality and validate acceptance criteria throughout the development lifecycle. They collaborate with developers and product managers to define test strategies, execute testing plans, and report quality metrics.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain test plans and QA approach for each project phase
- Validate acceptance criteria and sign-off on feature readiness
- Execute manual and automated testing across unit, integration, and end-to-end levels
- Report quality metrics, defect trends, and release readiness
- Collaborate with developers to improve test coverage and CI/CD pipelines

### Goals
- Ensure product meets quality and acceptance standards before release
- Reduce post-release defects and improve customer experience
- Continuously improve testing efficiency and coverage

### Typical Communication
- Quality metrics in weekly status updates
- Test plan reviews with product and development teams
- Release readiness reports and sign-off

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Partner on test automation strategies, code coverage goals, and defect remediation
- **Product Managers**: Validate acceptance criteria alignment and prioritize quality testing efforts
- **Project Managers**: Provide release readiness assessments and quality metrics for milestone tracking

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects design scalable, maintainable systems and provide technical guidance on complex decisions. They partner with developers and product leads to evaluate trade-offs and ensure long-term technical health.

### Responsibilities
- Design and review technical solutions for major features or systems
- Evaluate architectural trade-offs (scalability, performance, maintainability)
- Define technical standards, patterns, and best practices
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Mentor developers and support capability building

### Goals
- Deliver maintainable, scalable systems that support business goals
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Build team technical capabilities

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Trade-off assessments with product and project leads
- Technical guidance in code reviews and mentoring

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Provide architectural guidance, code review feedback, and technical mentoring on design decisions
- **Product Managers**: Advise on technical feasibility and trade-offs impacting roadmap and scope
- **Project Managers**: Identify and escalate technical risks and dependencies affecting timelines
- **QA/Testing Leads**: Consult on testability requirements and performance benchmarks

---

## Sponsor/Stakeholder Representative

### Role Summary
Sponsors/Stakeholder Representatives provide business context, decision-making authority, and resource allocation for projects. They ensure alignment between project outcomes and organizational priorities.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project charters and prioritization decisions
- Provide business context and success criteria
- Remove organizational blockers and escalate resource constraints
- Participate in major milestone reviews and go/no-go decisions
- Communicate project status to upper leadership and affected teams

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value
- Align projects with organizational strategy
- Enable team success through timely decision-making and resource allocation

### Typical Communication
- Milestone reviews and decision points
- Executive status updates and health indicators
- Escalation and resource coordination

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Project Managers**: Set expectations, make go/no-go decisions, and unblock resource constraints
- **Product Managers**: Provide business context and align success metrics with organizational priorities
- **Developers & Technical Architects**: Understand technical risks and trade-offs impacting business outcomes
